Questions

BE CLEAR BEFORE YOU CONNECT.

Factual answers, conservative claims, and no hidden “magic”.

What is IRIS?

IRIS is an ORVIA technology intended to sit above authorised business systems, information and specialist digital workers, helping people ask, coordinate, review and decide from one place.

Does IRIS replace my existing systems?

Not by default. The design principle is progressive connection to suitable existing systems rather than a forced rip-and-replace migration.

Which AI models can IRIS work with?

The architecture may work with leading AI technologies and specialist services where suitable integrations, permissions and configuration are available. Individual capabilities must be verified before being presented as live.

Do I need to move all of my data?

No blanket migration is assumed. Onboarding should identify where trusted information already lives and connect only what is appropriate.

Can IRIS make decisions for me?

IRIS can prepare, analyse and recommend. Consequential actions and important decisions remain subject to appropriate human approval.

Can different staff have different permissions?

Yes, the platform direction includes organisation-specific users and permissions. Exact controls depend on the deployed configuration.

Can IRIS work with Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace?

These are target integration environments. Actual availability depends on the customer's licences, permissions, configuration and verified connector capability.

How is customer information separated?

The product direction is multi-tenant: separate customer environments with their own users, permissions, systems, data, agents, documents and operational context.

Does ORVIA have access to everything?

No blanket access should be assumed. Access depends on the configured service, permissions and technical architecture agreed for the customer environment.

Can I remove a connection?

Connections should be administratively controllable, subject to the capabilities of the underlying platform and agreed configuration.

Can IRIS support an ORVIA Business in a Box?

Yes. IRIS is intended to be configurable as the operating-intelligence layer supplied with an ORVIA-developed business.

How long does onboarding take?

There is no single published duration yet. The proposed journey is Discover, Connect, Map, Configure, Verify, Go Live and then Expand.
