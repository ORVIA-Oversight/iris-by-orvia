About IRIS

HUMANS DO HUMAN.
MACHINES DO MACHINE.

IRIS is technology developed and operated by ORVIA Oversight Ltd. It exists to remove unnecessary machine-like work from people and return time for judgement, relationships, leadership, creativity and life.

Why IRIS exists

YOUR PEOPLE SHOULD NOT HAVE TO HOLD THE SYSTEM TOGETHER.

Modern businesses accumulated email, CRM, documents, cloud storage, phones, finance, calendars, task systems, AI tools, dashboards and automations. IRIS exists because the human should not have to become the integration layer.

Machines are good at

Searching, sorting, checking, monitoring, comparing, organising, summarising, routing and repetitive administration.

Humans are better placed for

Judgement, relationships, leadership, empathy, creativity, responsibility, values, care and decision-making.

The deeper principle

Work to live. Don't live to work. Technology should create capacity for people to live better lives, not simply increase the amount of work they can survive.
